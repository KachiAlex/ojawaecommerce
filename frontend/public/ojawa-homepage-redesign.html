How escrow works

Four stamps, one safe delivery

Every Ojawa order moves through the same checkpoints — like a parcel crossing borders. Your payment only clears each checkpoint when you say it's safe to.

Checkpoint 01

Place your order

Pay through Ojawa. Your money is booked into escrow — it doesn't go to the seller yet.

Checkpoint 02

Payment held

We hold your funds securely while the vendor prepares and ships your order.

Checkpoint 03

Track delivery

Follow your order from the vendor's warehouse to your door, wherever you are.

Checkpoint 04

Confirm & release

Only once you confirm the item arrived as expected do we release payment to the vendor.
